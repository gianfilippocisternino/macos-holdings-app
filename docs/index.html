What Holdings tracks

Investments

Stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto — live prices from Yahoo Finance, automatically classified by quoteType and Morningstar category.

Cash & deposits

Checking, savings, term deposits in any currency. Totals are reconciled in your display currency with live FX rates.

Real estate

Properties with OMI valuations for Italy and HPI-based estimates for the US, Germany, and Spain. Track multiple homes across countries.

Mortgages & debts

Link a mortgage to its property so net worth reflects the leverage. Variable-rate mortgages get an automatic review prompt.

Multi-currency

Twelve+ display currencies. Each asset keeps its native currency; totals convert at current rates with manual refresh.

Monthly history

Every asset records a value per month. Charts and category breakdowns show how your net worth shifts over time.

Private by design

Your financial data stays on this device and syncs only through your private iCloud account. No accounts to create, no servers holding your data. Anonymous usage analytics (no advertising identifier, no personal data) help us improve the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Holdings uses Core Data with iCloud CloudKit sync — your data lives in your private iCloud account, never on our servers. We don't have user accounts or a backend database.

Where do live prices come from?

Yahoo Finance for stocks, ETFs, bonds, and crypto. FX rates come from Yahoo as well. Real-estate valuations use OMI data from Italy's Agenzia delle Entrate and Eurostat/FRED house-price indexes for other countries.

Does Holdings collect any data?

Anonymous usage analytics through Firebase Analytics — which screens you visit, when you add or remove assets, when you change currency. No advertising identifier, no personal data, no portfolio contents. See the privacy policy for details.

Is Holdings free?

Yes — all core tracking is free. Holdings Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase that unlocks geography breakdown, real-estate valuations, net-worth goals, and PDF export. No subscriptions.

What devices does Holdings run on?

Mac (macOS 15.6 or later) and iPhone/iPad (iOS 18.6 or later). One purchase covers both, with Family Sharing supported.
