Privacy

Private by design — and by architecture.

There's no Holdings account. There's no Holdings backend. Your portfolio lives inside your Apple ID's encrypted iCloud, where only you and your devices can read it. We literally cannot see your data, because we don't have it.

Your iCloud, your data

Everything is stored in your private iCloud container via Apple's CloudKit. Sync between your Mac, iPad and iPhone happens directly — without us in the middle.

No servers, no accounts

There is no login, no signup, no email. We don't have a database holding your balances or your tickers. There's nothing for us — or anyone else — to breach.

Encrypted end-to-end by Apple

CloudKit data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with keys tied to your Apple ID. Even Apple's storage operators can't read it. Neither can we.

No advertising, no data brokers

No advertising identifier. No third-party SDKs that resell your data. Anonymous analytics record screen views and event counts — never values, tickers, or who you are.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best way to track net worth without a spreadsheet?

Use a dedicated net-worth app instead of Excel or Google Sheets. Holdings keeps a live total of every asset — investments, cash, real estate, vehicles and debts — fetching market prices and FX rates automatically, so there are no formulas to maintain and no monthly copy-paste. It also values your property from official datasets, something a spreadsheet can't do, and keeps everything private in your own iCloud.

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No. Holdings never asks for bank credentials and uses no account-aggregation service like Plaid. Unlike Mint, Empower or Monarch, nothing is linked to your bank. You add the assets you want to track, and live prices come only from public market data (Yahoo Finance) and official property indexes.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud container — never on our servers. Holdings has no user accounts, no backend database, and no third-party storage. CloudKit handles sync, encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.

Can Holdings (or anyone else) see my portfolio?

No. We have no servers and no database that holds your data. Sync happens directly between your Apple devices through your encrypted iCloud. There is no way for us, or any third party, to access your balances or tickers — because we never receive them.

Where do live prices come from?

Yahoo Finance for stocks, ETFs, bonds, and crypto. FX rates come from Yahoo as well. Real-estate valuations use OMI data from Italy's Agenzia delle Entrate and Eurostat/FRED house-price indexes for other countries.

Does Holdings collect any data?

Anonymous usage analytics through Firebase — which screens are opened, when assets are added or removed, when the display currency changes. No advertising identifier, no personal data, no balances, no tickers. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Is Holdings free?

Yes — all core tracking is free, including the growth-rate dashboard. Holdings Pro is a one-time $19.99 purchase that unlocks geography breakdown, real-estate valuations, net-worth goals, and PDF export. No subscriptions.

What devices does Holdings run on?

Mac (macOS 15.6 or later), iPad (iPadOS 18.6 or later), and iPhone (iOS 18.6 or later). One purchase covers all three, with Family Sharing supported.
